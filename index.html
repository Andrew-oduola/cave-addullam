Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?+

Yes. There is no registration fee. You only need a phone, data and the commitment to take part.

Do I need an existing business?+

No. You can apply with an early idea, or with no clear idea yet. We will help you shape your starting point.

What if I miss a live session?+

Sessions are recorded and posted so you can catch up. The practical task helps you stay connected to the learning.

How much time do I need each week?+

Plan for one live session, a short lesson and time to complete a practical task. The exact schedule will be shared before the programme starts.

How are mentors matched?+

We look at your idea, interests and needs, then match you with an available mentor whose experience can help.
